Natural Earth (naturalearthdata.com)
=====================================

File: ne_110m_coastline.geojson
Source: Natural Earth, 1:110m cultural/physical vector data, "Coastline" layer.
Distribution used: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector
  (geojson/ne_110m_coastline.geojson, fetched 2026-08-30)

Coordinates rounded to 2 decimal places (~1 km at the equator) from the
distribution's original precision -- well below both this dataset's own
110m-scale resolution and anything a small on-screen map widget can
render a difference for. No features added, removed, or reprojected;
only the coordinate rounding above.
